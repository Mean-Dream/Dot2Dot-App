4. Children's Data

WonderDot may be used by children under the supervision of a parent, legal guardian, teacher, or other responsible adult.

Children under 13 (or under 16 where required by applicable law): We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create an account independently. Account registration must be completed by a parent, guardian, or supervising adult, who accepts responsibility for the child's use of the App.

Where required by law (including Article 8 of the GDPR), parental or guardian consent is required before a child under 16 can use features involving image uploads or account creation.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us at sam@mean-dream.com and we will delete that data promptly.

What we do with children's data:

  • We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service.
  • We do not use children's data for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.
  • We do not share children's data with third parties except as necessary to operate the App (see Section 7).
