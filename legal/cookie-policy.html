1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website or use a web-based application. They allow the App to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and understand how you use the service.

We also use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage, which work in a comparable way. References to "cookies" in this policy include these technologies unless stated otherwise.

2. Who Sets Cookies

Cookies on WonderDot are set either by us (first-party cookies) or by our trusted third-party service providers (third-party cookies). We only allow third-party cookies from the processors listed in our Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies We Do Not Use

We do not use:

5. Your Choices

5.1 Cookie Consent Banner

When you first use WonderDot (web), you will be shown a cookie consent banner. You can accept all cookies, or customise your preferences by category. Strictly Necessary cookies cannot be disabled. You can update your preferences at any time via Settings → Privacy → Cookie Preferences in the App.

5.2 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view and delete existing cookies, block cookies, or set preferences. Guidance for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5.3 Mobile Apps

On mobile, cookies are not used in the same way. Our mobile app uses device storage and the RevenueCat SDK to manage subscriptions. You can manage app permissions and data through your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → WonderDot).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or change the technologies we use. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be notified via the App or by email.
